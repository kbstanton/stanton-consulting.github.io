Led by Kevin Stanton, Ph.D.

Stanton Consulting is a solo practice led by Kevin Stanton. His work has included defining major parts of IEEE 802.1 Time-Sensitive Networking, contributing to IEEE 1588/PTP, and leading timekeeping efforts within Intel across CPU timekeeping, PCIe Precision Time Measurement, and Linux support.

That combination is unusual: standards leadership, platform architecture, silicon-aware timing design, and the ability to communicate specialized technical issues clearly to both engineers and non-specialists.

He also founded the TSN/A Conference in 2016 and helped found Avnu Alliance.

Available for consulting engagements, workshops, technical reviews, standards-related strategy, and expert witness work involving synchronization, timing, and computer timekeeping.
